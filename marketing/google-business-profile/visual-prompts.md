# Recursos visuales generados

Modo utilizado: herramienta integrada `image_gen`.

Estas imágenes son recursos editoriales genéricos. No representan clientes, resultados ni oficinas reales de Domi Websites. Los proyectos del calendario usan sus capturas existentes.

## `web-design-editorial.png`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: Google Business Profile post image for a St. Louis web design studio
Primary request: candid editorial photo of a small-business owner reviewing a modern website on a laptop and smartphone at a tidy worktable
Scene/backdrop: authentic small office with subtle St. Louis red-brick character visible through a softly blurred window
Subject: one adult business owner, natural posture, hands using the laptop, phone beside it
Style/medium: photorealistic editorial business photography with believable screen glow and real material texture
Composition/framing: square-friendly medium shot, uncluttered, clear subject, enough breathing room for social cropping
Lighting/mood: soft natural morning light, capable and optimistic, not staged
Color palette: neutral charcoal and warm wood with subtle cyan accents
Constraints: no readable screen text, no logos, no fake interface details, no watermark, no exaggerated luxury
Avoid: generic corporate stock-photo smiles, extra fingers, overly polished AI aesthetic, neon cyberpunk styling
```
